Dashboard

What needs you now, every job by importance, the numbers, and career coaching – protected by your password.

No history has been saved yet. To switch it on:

  1. Open the repository's Actions secrets and add one named DASHBOARD_PASSWORD. Use a long passphrase – it's the only thing protecting your history.
  2. Press Run (▶ at the top of this page) → Run now. Today's jobs, and the statistics so far, are saved encrypted.
  3. Come back here and type the same password to unlock.

Experience levels

Only jobs at the levels you switch on are sent. A job's level comes from its title, then “N years of experience”, then the job site (and the AI reviewer, if on).

Also send jobs that don't say a levelMany ads don't – recommended

Qualification

The bot reads each advert – text, poster or PDF – and notes what it asks for. A job is sent when it accepts a qualification you switch on, so “Degree or HND” still reaches a degree holder.

Also send jobs that don't sayOr whose poster couldn't be read – recommended

EEE sub-fields

A job is sent when it matches a switched-on sub-field. Tap a row to edit its keywords – whole words, and a trailing * matches any ending (electric* → electrical, electricity).

Filters

Drop jobs you never want, and limit where they are.

Press Enter after each word. Add technician and electrician for engineer-level roles only.

Company names follow the same rules as keywords: whole words, and * matches any ending (SLT*).
