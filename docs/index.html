🎓 Experience levels

Only jobs at the levels you switch on are sent. The level comes from the job title, then from “N years of experience” in the ad, then from the job site (and from the AI reviewer if it's on).

⚡ EEE sub-fields

A job is sent if it matches a switched-on sub-field. Keywords: one per line, whole words, a trailing * matches any ending (electric* → electrical, electricity…).
